Index of all Roles

qumulo.cluster

  • qumulo.cluster.cluster_baseline – Bring a Qumulo cluster to the baseline its inventory declares

  • qumulo.cluster.cluster_upgrade – Upgrade a fleet of Qumulo clusters hop by hop, in the order the upgrade plan gives

  • qumulo.cluster.edge_cache_resize – Bring a Qumulo edge’s cache to a target size, by the least disruptive route

  • qumulo.cluster.edge_rebuild – Rebuild a Qumulo unprotected edge, draining it first, and bring the new edge back to its baseline

  • qumulo.cluster.portal_pair – Pair a spoke cluster with a hub cluster through a portal, or drain and decommission the pairing

  • qumulo.cluster.replication_failover – Fail a replication relationship over to its target, copy the target’s writes back, or fail it back

  • qumulo.cluster.replication_pair – Declare a replication relationship between two clusters, or remove it, refusing before any write
