Index of all Filter Plugins

qumulo.cluster

  • qumulo.cluster.artifact_dir – Where the collection’s roles keep a host’s records and secrets

  • qumulo.cluster.host_vars_duplicates – A host_vars file’s variables without the shares and exports the inventory already declares

  • qumulo.cluster.item_areas – The keys inside list items that put a declared variable in an area

  • qumulo.cluster.policy_lock_keys – What the cluster_baseline role applies of the snapshot policies, given the key store

  • qumulo.cluster.redact_images – An upgrade images list without the secret parts of its URLs

  • qumulo.cluster.restore_key_hash – The digest that names a relationship’s restore record and restore stamp

  • qumulo.cluster.restore_report_claims – The rebuild report’s claim on a host’s relationships, read on the controller

  • qumulo.cluster.tenancy_first_pass – What the edge_rebuild replay declares in its tenant 1 pass, before the baseline

  • qumulo.cluster.tenancy_status_due – Whether the cluster_baseline role’s step 2 reads the cluster’s network status

  • qumulo.cluster.url_secret – The secret parts of an upgrade image’s URL

  • qumulo.cluster.warn – Show a text as a warning of the task that renders it
